FAQs

What are your hours of operation?
Monday–Thursday: 11:00 AM – 9:00 PM
Friday–Saturday: 11:00 AM – 10:00 PM
Sunday: 12:00 PM – 8:00 PM

Is parking available?
Yes! Complimentary on-site parking is available for all guests, with additional street parking nearby.

Do you have outdoor seating?
Yes. Our outdoor patio is open seasonally and offers a relaxing atmosphere for enjoying Mediterranean cuisine.

Is Olive & Vine family-friendly?
Absolutely! We welcome guests of all ages and offer a children's menu, high chairs, and a comfortable dining environment for families.

Do you offer gift cards?
Yes. Gift cards are available in any amount and can be purchased in the restaurant.

Do I need a reservation?
Reservations are recommended for dinner service and weekends, but walk-in guests are always welcome based on availability.

Can I make a reservation online?
Yes! Reservations can be made through our website or by calling the restaurant during business hours.

How do I cancel or modify my reservation?
Reservations may be changed or canceled online or by contacting us at least 24 hours before your scheduled dining time.

Do you offer vegetarian or vegan options?
Yes. Our menu features a variety of vegetarian and vegan dishes, including falafel, hummus, fresh salads, and vegetable-based entrees.

Can you accommodate food allergies?
Absolutely. Please let your server know about any allergies or dietary restrictions, and we'll do our best to accommodate your needs.

Do you have gluten-free options?
Yes. Several menu items can be prepared gluten-free upon request.

Do you offer takeout?
Yes! Every item on our menu is available for takeout. Orders can be placed online or by phone.

Do you offer delivery?
Yes. Delivery is available within our local service area through our online ordering system.

Do you host private events?
Yes. Our private dining space is perfect for birthdays, anniversaries, business dinners, family gatherings, and other special occasions.

How many guests can private events accommodate?
Our private event space comfortably accommodates up to 60 guests. Contact us for availability and event planning details.
